The studio

First institutional capital, at the founding round

Aurelian Ventures is a New York studio that invests at the founding round of teams rebuilding the foundational layers of technology. We know these founders directly, and have spent the past year working alongside them as they build and deploy.

That proximity lets us commit early, as the first institutional capital these teams take, before they are widely visible.

What we back

The layer the rest is built on

We invest one layer beneath the applications and platforms most capital competes for, in the infrastructure that other systems come to depend on. Foundational, not featural.

Compute

Making intelligence cheaper at the source

Teams taking requirements the industry treats as fixed and reducing them, bending the economics that govern data centers and AI.

Settlement

Making institutional transactions provable

A protocol earning the trust of the institutions that ran the prior settlement rails, rebuilding proof and settlement for banks and, increasingly, for machines.

Different industries, different teams, one underlying shift

Focus

A deliberately narrow mandate

We hold a small number of positions, at one layer of the stack, with founders we know well. The narrowness is the point. Depth and proximity are the advantage, and neither scales.

Held closely, these companies increasingly reinforce one another, a pattern more visible from inside the portfolio than from outside it.

We would rather own the foundation than rent the surface

The Studio

An operating studio, built for the foundation layer

The foundational layers of technology are being rebuilt, and the teams doing it are reachable to those already close to the work.

Until now, Aurelian Ventures has deployed its own time and capital alongside these teams. Fund I formalizes that work into an investment vehicle, so a small group of families and institutions can invest at the founding round rather than read about these companies later.
