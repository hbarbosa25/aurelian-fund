    Privacy notice

    Version 1.0 · 6 August 2026

    Who we are

    Aurelian Ventures, New York. For anything on this page, write to contact@aurelianlabs.ai.

    What we collect

    Only what you type into the incubator application form: your name, email, role, LinkedIn profile, and the information you choose to give about your company. This site sets no cookies and runs no analytics or tracking of any kind.

    Fonts on this site are served by Google Fonts, which receives your IP address as part of loading them.

    Why we use it

    To evaluate your company for the incubator and to contact you about your application. Nothing else. We do not sell it, we do not share it outside Aurelian, and we do not add applicants to any mailing list.

    How long we keep it

    Applications are deleted 24 months after they are submitted, unless we are working with you by then.

    Confidentiality

    Submitting an application does not create an NDA or any duty of confidentiality. We review many companies and may already be working with, or later back, one similar to yours. Send only what you are comfortable sharing.

    Your rights

    You can ask us for a copy of what you sent, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it. Email contact@aurelianlabs.ai and we will act on it. If you are in the UK or EU, you also have the right to complain to your data protection authority.
